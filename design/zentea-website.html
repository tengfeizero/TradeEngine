Our Tea Garden

Where Clouds Meet
the Tea Plants

Nestled in the misty mountains of Zhejiang Province at 1,200 meters elevation, our 800-hectare tea garden has been cultivated by the same family for three generations. The persistent cloud cover diffuses sunlight, slowing leaf growth and concentrating the amino acids that give our tea its signature sweetness.

Every leaf is hand-plucked by our 200+ skilled pickers during the narrow spring window, then immediately transported to our on-site processing facility — ensuring freshness from bush to cup within hours.
